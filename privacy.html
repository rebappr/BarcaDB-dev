Introduction

Barça Database respects the privacy of its visitors and is committed to protecting any information that may be processed while using the website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when visiting the website and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

Barça Database does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, postal addresses, or payment information.

Like most websites, our web hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information through server logs, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages visited

This information is used solely for technical, security, and operational purposes.

Cookies

Barça Database does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or visitor tracking.

How Information Is Used

Any information processed through server logs is used exclusively to:

  • Maintain and secure the website
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Monitor website performance
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized access

Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites. Barça Database is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites separately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the updated revision date.
